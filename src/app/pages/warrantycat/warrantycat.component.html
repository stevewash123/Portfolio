WarrantyCat

In private beta

WarrantyCat helps you keep track of return windows and warranty expirations for the things you buy, and reminds you before they close. It's currently in private testing.

Contact: support@microsaasbuilders.com

SMS reminders

WarrantyCat users can choose to get text message reminders about upcoming return and warranty deadlines. To opt in, a user checks a box in their notification settings and enters their mobile number. Texting is optional and never required to use the app. Message frequency varies, typically 1 to 5 messages a month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.
