From Disconnected System Chaos to Clarity

A Technical Decision Guide for Growing Businesses

By Stephen Wash

Purpose of This Guide

If you're running a growing business, you've probably hit the point where spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual processes are costing you time and money. But custom software feels expensive and risky—and you're not sure if it's the right answer. This guide helps you make that decision with confidence.

Over 28 years of building software for businesses, I've seen the same patterns repeat. Business owners make expensive mistakes—not because they're careless, but because they lack the right information at the right time. This guide provides that information: when custom software makes sense, what it actually costs, and how to avoid the common pitfalls.

Whether you decide to build custom software, stick with off-the-shelf tools, or use a hybrid approach, this guide will help you make the best decision for your specific situation.

Key Highlights

💰 What Business IT Solutions Actually Cost

Understanding your options means understanding real costs. Whether you're considering off-the-shelf tools, custom automation, or full applications, here's what different solutions actually cost—and how they compare.

Business IT Solution Cost Comparison

Examples of Each Category:
  • Small Automation: Automated report generation, data sync between systems, simple integrations
  • Forms & Data Collection: Custom intake forms, survey systems, lead capture
  • Workflow Systems: More complex integrations, multi-system dashboards, scheduling systems
  • Full Applications: Fully customizable multi-user platform with backend service, data storage, and professional UI
  • Off-the-Shelf Suite: Salesforce (CRM) + QuickBooks (accounting) + Slack (communication) - 3-year subscription, implementation, and training costs

Note: Custom solutions often have lower ongoing costs than off-the-shelf subscriptions, especially for growing teams.
